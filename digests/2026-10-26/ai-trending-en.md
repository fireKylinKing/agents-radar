The clearest explosive signal is not a new foundation model but tooling around coding agents. Cloudflare’s `security-audit-skill`, `Nutlope/hallmark`, `pbakaus/impeccable`, and `ECC` package security review, design quality, and harness optimization as reusable skills for Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and similar CLIs. This is becoming a distribution layer: “agent skills” and harness upgrades ship as discrete repos, with daily stars rivaling full applications. A second signal is local-first AI applications: `VoiceStudio`’s +1,592 daily stars shows demand for self-hosted voice cloning, dubbing, and transcription, not just cloud APIs. Infrastructure attention is shifting toward context and memory: `claude-mem`, `mem0`, `headroom`, `cognee`, `Graphify`, `RAGFlow`, and `LightRAG` attack token pressure, persistence, and retrieval quality. RAG is broadening beyond vector search into graph-based codebase indexing, vectorless reasoning retrieval, and compression proxies. New stacks are visible in Rust terminal agents and embedded retrieval (`Codewhale`, `rig`, `qdrant`, `lancedb`), TypeScript frontend agent stacks (`CopilotKit`), and Go RAG/agent servers. The model layer remains multi-provider—Ollama lists Kimi, GLM, MiniMax, DeepSeek, gpt-oss, Qwen, and Gemma—so differentiation is moving to skills, memory, security, and UX. Daily trending data also shows vertical agents maturing in finance, job search, stock analysis, presentations, and SEO, where domain workflows are packaged as installable skills or CLI-native assistants. Classic ML frameworks remain foundational but are not driving today’s daily momentum; the excitement is at the orchestration and application layer.
